Who it's for and who it isn't

It's for you if…

You have customers who love you, but on Google you show up half-finished, with no direct bookings or few reviews. You want to be found, chosen and booked with no middleman.

It's not for you if…

  • Your salon is outside Barcelona and its metropolitan area. The photo session is in person; that's what the remote version is for. See Essential Care Remote
  • What you're after is a website (that's Step 2) or someone to look after your profile every month (Step 3).
  • You want someone to promise you the top spot on Google. Nobody can guarantee it, and we don't promise it.

What's included, explained

Your Google profile

  • A complete, well-kept Google Business Profile: categories, services, hours and description
    Your profile is what shows up on Google and Google Maps when someone searches for a salon like yours. We leave it complete, so whoever finds you knows what you do, when you're open and why to choose you.
  • Listing on Apple Maps and Bing
    Not everyone searches on Google: iPhone users often open Apple Maps. We list you there and on Bing too, with the same details, so your information matches everywhere.

Bookings with no middleman

  • "Book" button connected to your Booksy, Fresha or Treatwell
    We connect your booking system to your profile. That way, whoever finds you can book on the spot, without calling or messaging you.

Photos

  • 1-hour photo session at your premises: 10–12 edited photos
    We spend an hour at your salon and deliver between 10 and 12 edited photos. Real photos of your premises and your work build more trust than stock images.

Reviews

  • QR poster to collect reviews, plus reply templates
    A poster for your salon: your client scans the code and leaves a review on the spot. The templates save you working out what to reply to each one.

Results

  • Results report after 60 days
    After 60 days we show you what has changed, using the statistics from your own profile.

The photo session is in person in Barcelona and its metropolitan area. Outside that area, see Essential Care Remote.

Not included: The website (Step 2) or the monthly management of your profile (Step 3). We do not promise Google rankings.

FAQ

Do you guarantee I'll come first on Google or Google Maps?

No. Nobody can guarantee a ranking, and we don't promise one. What we do is leave your profile complete and well looked after, which is the foundation for being found.

My salon is outside Barcelona. Can I still hire it?

We only do the photo session in Barcelona and its metropolitan area. Outside that area there's Essential Care Remote: the same, without the photo session.

See Essential Care Remote

What is the founding price?

It's a reduced price for the first 5 spots. While spots remain, you'll see it on this page; once they're gone, the standard price applies: €450 + VAT.

How do I pay?

Payment: 50% at the start and 50% on delivery, by bank transfer and with an invoice for each payment.

Is there a lock-in period?

No. It's a one-off project with no lock-in. The profile, the photos and the logins are always yours.
