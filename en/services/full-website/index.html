Who it's for and who it isn't

It's for you if…

Your business has lots of services to show and you want each one to have its place on the website and be easy to find.

It's not for you if…

  • A single page is enough for you. The One-page Website costs less. See One-page Website
  • You want an online shop with lots of products. That's quoted separately.

What's included, explained

Your website

  • Up to 6 pages
    Instead of squeezing everything onto a single page, we spread your information across up to six, so your salon gets the room it needs.

Your services

  • One page for each key service
    Each important service has its own page. That way, anyone looking for exactly that service in your area can land straight on it.

Search engines and AI

  • FAQs and structured data for search engines and AI
    On your website we answer what your clients always ask, and we add organised information that search engines and AI assistants can read. It doesn't guarantee they'll mention you: it makes you easy to understand.

Included

  • 30 min of La Trastienda
    Half an hour of our business consulting, included.

Not included: An online shop with lots of products is quoted separately.

FAQ

Why is the price “from”?

Because it depends on your services. We prepare a custom quote starting at €1,290 + VAT (€1,560.90 VAT incl.).

How is it different from the One-page Website?

The One-page Website tells everything on a single page. The Full Website has up to 6, with one page per key service and an FAQ.

See One-page Website

Do you guarantee that Google or ChatGPT will recommend my salon?

No. Nobody controls an AI's answers or Google's rankings, so we don't promise it: we lay the foundations.

Does it include an online shop?

An online shop with lots of products is quoted separately.

How do I pay?

Payment: 50% at the start and 50% on delivery, by bank transfer and with an invoice for each payment.

Is there a lock-in period?

No. It's a one-off project with no lock-in. The website, the domain and the logins are always yours.
