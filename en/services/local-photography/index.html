Who it's for and who it isn't

It's for you if…

Your photos are dark, old or stock images, and they don't show how lovely your business is or how good your work is.

It's not for you if…

  • You're after video, models or special equipment. That's quoted separately.
  • Your business is outside the Barcelona area. Travel is quoted separately.

What's included, explained

Before the shoot

  • Shot list designed for your website, Google Maps and social media
    Before we come, we decide which photos are needed and what for, so we don't improvise on the day.
  • Tips to get your premises, products and team ready
    We tell you how to get everything ready, so the session time goes on taking photos.

Shoot at your premises

  • Up to 2 hours at your business, with art direction
    Art direction means we take care over what appears in each photo and how: we don't just turn up, shoot and leave.
  • Space, shopfront, team at work and star products or work
    The shopfront helps people recognise you when they arrive; the interior, the team and your work show what coming to your salon is like.

Delivery

  • About 30 edited photos (light, colour and framing)
    We don't hand over everything that comes out of the camera: we deliver around 30 photos, already edited.
  • Formatted for your website, Google Maps and social media (landscape and portrait)
    Every platform wants a different shape of photo. We give you landscape and portrait versions so you don't have to crop them.
  • Usage rights forever
    The photos are yours: you can use them wherever and whenever you like.

Not included: Video, models, special equipment hire or travel outside the Barcelona area (quoted separately).

How we work

  1. Before
    Short call and shot list.
  2. Shoot day
    Up to 2 hours at your premises, at the time with the best light.
  3. 1-2 weeks later
    Delivery of the edited photos.

Timing: Delivery in 1 to 2 weeks

What we need from you

  • Your premises ready and tidy
  • Products or work ready to be photographed
  • Permission from anyone who appears in the photos

Price

€250 + VAT (€302.50 VAT incl.)

Business prices, fixed for the scope described. VAT (21%) not included; the VAT-inclusive total is shown. 50% on start, 50% on delivery. No lock-in.

FAQ

How is it different from the Essential Care photo session?

The Essential Care one lasts 1 hour and gives you 10–12 edited photos. Local Photography is a session of up to 2 hours, with around 30 edited photos.

See Essential Care

Do you do sessions outside Barcelona?

Travel outside the Barcelona area is quoted separately.

Does it include video?

No. Video, models and special equipment hire are quoted separately.

Who owns the photos?

You do, with usage rights forever.

How do I pay?

Payment: 50% at the start and 50% on delivery, by bank transfer and with an invoice for each payment.
