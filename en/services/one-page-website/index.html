Who it's for and who it isn't

It's for you if…

You want your own shopfront online: clear, fast and with your booking button, with no hassle.

It's not for you if…

  • You have lots of services and want each one to have its own page. That's what the Full Website is for. See Full Website
  • You want an online shop with lots of products. That's quoted separately.

What's included, explained

Your page

  • One clear, fast, mobile-first page
    All your information on a single page, made to read well on a phone. Instagram helps people fall for you, but Google and AI assistants mostly read websites and business profiles; and your website is yours, it doesn't depend on an algorithm.
  • Booksy or Fresha booking button built in
    Your page carries your booking system's button, so whoever visits can book from there.

Changes

  • 2 rounds of changes
    We show you a first version and you can ask us for changes up to twice before we publish it.

Domain and hosting

  • Domain and hosting for the first year
    The domain is your website's address and hosting is where it lives. Both are included for the first year; from the second year, renewing the domain is up to you.

Not included: From the second year, renewing the domain is up to you. If you need several pages or one page for each service, look at the Full Website.

FAQ

I have Instagram. Do I really need a website?

Yes. Instagram helps people fall in love with you, but Google and AI assistants mostly read websites and business listings. A simple, fast, well-structured website is the official source they all draw from, and it's yours: it doesn't depend on an algorithm.

Who owns the website?

You do. The website, the domain and the logins are always in your name.

What happens with the domain from the second year?

The first year of domain and hosting is included. From the second year, renewing the domain is up to you.

How much is it if I'm already an Essential Care client?

€250 + VAT (€302.50 VAT incl.), instead of €690 + VAT.

What if I need more than one page?

Then the Full Website is the right fit: up to 6 pages, with one page per key service.

How do I pay?

Is there a lock-in period?

No. It's a one-off project with no lock-in.
