    How do I get ChatGPT, Gemini or Google's AI to recommend my business?

    AI assistants recommend businesses they can find information about that is clear, consistent and verified in many places: your website, your Google Maps listing, reviews, directories and local mentions. We work on those sources so AI understands what you do, where and for whom, and can take you into account when someone asks about your service in your area. Nobody controls an AI's answers, so we don't promise they'll name you: we lay the foundations.

    What is local SEO and why does a small business need it?

    It's the set of actions that make you show up when someone looks for what you sell near them, for example “hair salon near me”. It includes your Google Maps listing, reviews, your website and consistent data online. For a neighbourhood shop it's usually the most profitable source of customers.

    How long does it take to notice?

    A well-optimised Google Maps listing usually starts improving within a few weeks. Rankings in search engines and in AI answers build up over time and usually consolidate within 3 to 6 months, depending on your sector and the competition in your area.

    I have Instagram. Do I really need a website?

    Yes. Instagram helps people fall in love with you, but Google and AI assistants mostly read websites and business listings. A simple, fast, well-structured website is the official source they all draw from, and it's yours: it doesn't depend on an algorithm.
