    What people always ask us.

    Can't find your question? Write to us and we'll reply within 48 working hours.

    Do I need a website if I already have Instagram or Booksy?

    Not to start with. But your Instagram doesn't show up when someone searches "hair salon near me"; your Google profile does. If you don't have a website, the New shop window brings it all together.

    Do you work with Booksy, Fresha or Treatwell?

    Yes. We connect your booking button to the platform you already use.

    When will I notice?

    The profile is usually the first thing to move; the website and AI are slower. We don't promise timelines or rankings: we show you what has changed at 60 days.

    How do I get ChatGPT or Google to recommend me?

    Nobody can guarantee it. What does help: clear, consistent, up-to-date information on your profile, your website and your listings. That's what we look after.

    How much does it cost?

    From €250 + VAT (€302.50 in total). All prices are on the house menu.

    How do I pay?

    50% upfront and 50% on delivery, by bank transfer.

    Is there a lock-in period?

    No.

    Do you come to the salon?

    Only for the photo shoot, in Barcelona and the metropolitan area. Everything else is online. Outside Barcelona, we work remotely.

    What do I need to do?

    Give us access to your profile and 1 hour for the photos. We take care of the rest.
